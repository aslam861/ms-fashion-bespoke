ABSTRACT

M.S Fashion: Bespoke Ethnic Menswear Platform is a technology-driven platform designed to simplify, modernize, and elevate bespoke tailoring consultations for high-end Indian ethnic menswear. Clients seeking wedding and celebration attire—such as royal Sherwanis, aristocratic Jodhpuri Bandhgalas, Indo-Western fusion silhouettes, and bespoke Tuxedos—often face difficulties with generic factory sizing, unorganized communication, and fragmented record-keeping. The proposed system provides a centralized digital atelier platform where users can explore curated collections, inspect intricate hand-embroidery with interactive zoom calibration, configure royal accessories, calculate itemized price estimations, and book verified private fittings.

The system incorporates an interactive hardware-accelerated CSS Transform-Origin Visual Zoom Calibrator (2.2x zoom) that allows clients to examine fine Zardozi wirework, brocade textures, and accessory details. To solve high-ticket bridal wear communication challenges, the application integrates an intelligent Royal Atelier AI Stylist Chatbot providing contextual styling advice for wedding events, explaining the 15+ body measurement process, and routing inquiries directly to the Master Craftsman on WhatsApp (+91 89993 86222) with automated garment photo links. A two-step royal Email OTP verification protocol powered by Gmail SMTP safeguards the boutique from appointment spam while enabling a client-defined Expected Budget (Self Budget) entry.

By integrating modern web technology, an AI conversational assistant, and centralized master administrative controls, the proposed system makes bespoke ethnic menswear tailoring more organized, accessible, transparent, and proactive, improving the overall couture experience for clients and master tailors alike.
